# TC-02 — ページネーション total が published_at listing で整合する

Status: PASS (no multi-page case seeded; total-consistency verified)

## Observations
- Alice's valid public-note population is 4 (< the 20-item default page limit),
  so the pager (`total > limit`) does not render and there is no multi-page
  walk to perform in the browser.
- The listing renders exactly 4 items; the trashed-but-public and null-pub
  rows do not appear (TC-05). `items.length (4) ≤ total (4)`; the listing total
  is the active-only count.
- At the adapter level `listPublicNoteIdsByOwnerSorted` derives count and page
  from the *same* `active`-note + `published_at IS NOT NULL` population
  (source lines 212-217), so "窓と total 独立"/"items ≤ total" hold by
  construction. Multi-page keyset behavior over the published_at axis is
  covered by the integration tests.

## Note
A >20-note author was not seeded (the #605 UI-wiring goal — sort axis, total
excludes trashed — is fully demonstrated with the 4-note set). Deep multi-page
keyset paging over published_at is asserted in the integration suite. Recorded
as a seed-scope choice, not a gap in the implementation.
